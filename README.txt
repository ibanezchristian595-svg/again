ANNIVERSARY WEBSITE — EASY SETUP

1. Open the "anniversary-website" folder in Visual Studio Code.
2. Open the "assets" folder, then open "images".
3. Copy your three pictures into that folder.
4. Rename them exactly:
   photo1.jpg
   photo2.jpg
   photo3.jpg
5. Do not delete photo-placeholder.svg. It appears when a photo is missing.
6. Open index.html in your browser. For the best experience, install the
   "Live Server" extension in VS Code, right-click index.html, then choose
   "Open with Live Server".

CUSTOMIZE:
- Change the message or names inside index.html.
- Change the anniversary/start dates near the top of script.js.
- The website currently uses September 18, 2024 as the relationship start date
  and September 18, 2026 as the second-anniversary date.

IMPORTANT:
- Keep index.html, style.css, and script.js together.
- Keep your photos inside assets/images.
- JPG/JPEG files renamed from another format may not work. If your photo is PNG,
  either convert it to JPG or change .jpg to .png in index.html.
